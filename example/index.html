Open decisions

  • Corner radius. --jrl-radius-ui is square (the current default theme); the old Astro primitives used 8px.
  • Text on accents is ink, not white. White on coral was 2.8:1 and white on teal 1.9:1; ink passes at 6.8 and 9.8.
  • The dark palette is a first pass. It's derived, not designed; the coral shadow is the main idea to keep or drop.
